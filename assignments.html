“Suggest stands” fills only the blank stands. It picks the smallest stand that fits the aircraft and is free, and offers an alternate. Stands you chose yourself are left alone.

Import flights

Bring in a CSV file, or copy rows from a spreadsheet and paste them below. Columns in this order: A/C type, Flight, Arr. (Int/Dom), ETA, ETD, Dep. (Int/Dom). A header row is fine and lets the columns be in any order. Write O/N in place of a time for an overnight aircraft. Imported flights are added to the ones already here.

Check-in counters, gates and baggage belts

Worked out from the schedule and the Rules tab. Type over any counter or pick another gate to change it; your changes show in bold and the rest is worked out around them. Clear a box to go back to automatic.
